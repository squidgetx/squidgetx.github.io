# Abstract

We examine a novel corpus of >80 RCTs testing the persuasiveness of political ads on Facebook ads and find no relationship between survey persuasion measures and common proxies for effectiveness like click-through-rate. However, we find emoji "reactions" are predictive of persuasion and use them to develop a surrogate metric.
